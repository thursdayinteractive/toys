# Build the turn tracker

A toy for classroom discussions, with two features (owner direction, 2026-09-28):
- **Turn timer.** Times each speaker's turn, to encourage letting others talk.
- **Talk clicker.** A person taps each time they lead or direct the discussion, so someone who overwhelms the conversation, or who is not taking part, becomes visible.

It will be the first toy to take the time from its host, which the repository spec already allows for ([[§5]]). Its core meets [[§3]].

Not yet answered:
- the toy's name on the menu, and its description;
- whether one device is passed around a group, or each person uses their own;
- how participants are entered, and whether a group can be saved;
- the turn length, whether it can be changed, and what happens when a turn runs out;
- whether click counts show live or only at the end, and whether they are kept;
- whether the two features share one screen.

## Done when
The owner has answered those questions, the toy's spec is written, and both features work in the standalone app.
